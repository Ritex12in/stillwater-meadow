# 0003. Story first, then a hook, then the video

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Who decided:** The owner's words, 2026-11-27.

## Context

Claude's first plan was a list of pretty shots (the seasons turning, a glide, a campfire). The owner: "you are missing ideas and story; the video can be about the progress of the game, like how the character changed over time"; and then: "a story, a hook, then video".

## Decision

A short begins as a story of something that really happened in the making of the game: a want, a difficulty, a turn, a result. The story is written first, with the commits that show it. Then the hook: the first two seconds, one picture and one line. Then the video that tells the story. A brief with no story is not ready.

## What follows

- HISTORY.md is the source of stories and is kept up to date.
- Pretty footage alone is not a short; it may be the picture under a story.
- Each short tells one thing.
