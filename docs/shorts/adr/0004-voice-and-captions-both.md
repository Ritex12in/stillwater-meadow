# 0004. A voice and captions, both

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Who decided:** The owner's decision, 2026-11-27. Whose voice: open.

## Context

Asked whether the shorts should have a voice or captions only, the owner said: "both voice and caption". Many people watch without sound; a voice makes the story personal for those who listen.

## Decision

Every short has a spoken voice and burned-in captions of the same words. The captions are big, a few words at a time, in the game's display font, timed to the voice. The short must make sense with the sound off.

## What follows

- Someone must speak. Whose voice is not decided: the owner's own, or a voice service (PLAN.md, open question 1). This PC has only the two built-in Windows voices, which are not good enough.
- A brief's script is written to be said aloud: short lines, about 2.5 words a second.
- The voice is recorded before filming is finished, so the cuts can follow it.
