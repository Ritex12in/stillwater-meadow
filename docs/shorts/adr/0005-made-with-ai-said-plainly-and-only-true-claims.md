# 0005. Made with AI, said plainly; and only true claims

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Who decided:** The owner asked for "everything is coded" to be mentioned; Claude found it untrue on 2026-11-27 and proposes this instead. Needs the owner's answer.

## Context

The owner: "mentioning made with AI is ok, also mentioning everything is coded". The first is simply true. The second is not true as the game stands: many trees, plants and rocks, the bench and fences, and the ground's textures are downloaded files (CC0), about 127 MB of them. Viewers who look at the game's files, or other developers, would find that in a minute, and a false claim would cost more than it gains.

## Decision

The shorts say openly that an AI wrote the code and the owner directed it. They say what is made in code where that is true (the characters, the animals, the buildings, the animation, the land, the water, the sky, the music) and never "everything", "100%" or "no assets" while downloaded files are in the game. CLAIMS.md lists what may and may not be said, checked against the code; every line of a script is checked against it before the brief is approved.

## What follows

- "Everything is coded" stays off the table unless the downloaded plants, rocks, props and ground textures are replaced by code-made ones (PLAN.md, open question 3).
- CLAIMS.md must be checked again when the game changes.
- This overrides the owner's wording on one point. It is put to the owner, not assumed.
