# 0009. Nothing is filmed before its brief is approved

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Who decided:** The owner's words, 2026-11-27.

## Context

Claude wrote a script and was about to start filming. The owner: "don't be eager to make video, let's set up a proper pipeline".

## Decision

No filming, rendering or editing of a short starts until its brief is marked `approved` by the owner. Until then the work is on paper: the story, the hook, the script, the shot list. Building and testing the tools is not filming, but is done only in the order PLAN.md gives and the owner has agreed.

## What follows

- Hours of rendering are not spent on a story the owner would change.
- The owner reads a page, not a video, to say yes or no.
- Trial footage to prove a tool works is allowed, and is thrown away.
