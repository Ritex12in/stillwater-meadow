# 0011. Only the game's own characters are in the story

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Who decided:** The owner ("stop mentioning external player assets, those are out of context").

## Context

The first brief told the character's story with a detour in it: characters from outside the game that
were tried for a day and removed. Claude wrote it into the script, the history and the ideas, and took
stills of it. The owner stopped the work: that detour is not part of the story they want told.

## Decision

The shorts, their briefs, and this folder's history and ideas tell the story of the game's own
characters only. Characters that were tried from outside are not shown, named or alluded to. Versions
of the game from the days when they were in it are filmed showing the game's own figures.

## What follows

- The first brief lost one line and two shots; its story runs from the first figure straight to the
  owner's drawings.
- A shot list must ask an old version for the game's own figures where that version would otherwise
  show something else, and stills are looked at before filming to make sure.
- This is about the characters in the shorts' story. What a video may claim about how the game is made
  is still governed by [CLAIMS.md](../CLAIMS.md).
