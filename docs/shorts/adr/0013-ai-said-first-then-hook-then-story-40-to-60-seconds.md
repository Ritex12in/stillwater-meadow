# 0013. "Built with AI" said first, then the hook, then the story; 40 to 60 seconds

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Who decided:** The owner's words, 2026-11-28. Supersedes the length in [0002](0002-vertical-short-and-safe.md) and the order on screen in [0003](0003-story-then-hook-then-video.md).

## Context

The first three shorts were 24 to 27 seconds long, each opening on its hook. The owner, having
seen them: "shorts videos are too short and missing some points; video should be of length 40 to
60 sec. Mention at start that I am building a game using AI completely, then a hook, then the story
continues."

## Decision

Every short is **40 to 60 seconds** long (about 100 to 140 spoken words at the voice's pace), and
is told in this order:

1. **The opener** (about 5 s): the same line every time, so a viewer knows at once what the channel
   is: "I'm building a whole game with AI. I haven't written a single line of code." Over a picture
   of the game as it is now, chosen for the short.
2. **The hook** (about 4 s): the short's own line and picture, as before.
3. **The story**, told in full: the want, the difficulty, the turn and the result, with the steps
   in between that the old 25-second cut had to skip.
4. **The end card**, as before.

The story is still written first ([0003](0003-story-then-hook-then-video.md) stands for that); only
the order on screen changes. Everything else in [0002](0002-vertical-short-and-safe.md) (1080 x
1920, the safe areas, one file for both platforms) stands.

## Why the opener is worded so

"Using AI completely" is said as "a whole game with AI; I haven't written a single line of code":
that is true ([CLAIMS.md](../CLAIMS.md): the AI writes all the code, the owner directs). Said as "AI
made everything", it would not be: many trees, plants and rocks, and the ground's pictures, are a
free downloaded kit ([0005](0005-made-with-ai-said-plainly-and-only-true-claims.md)).

## What follows

- The template's length is 40 to 60 seconds, and its script starts with the opener.
- Shorts 001, 002 and 003 are rewritten to this and made again.
- A story that does not fill 40 seconds is not yet a story: it needs its steps, or it joins another.
