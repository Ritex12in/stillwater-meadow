## The story

On 13 November the game was one hand-placed valley: a meadow, a lake, mountains painted round it
(`620f25a`). On 21 November the owner asked for a world made from a seed, without an edge, and the
valley was replaced by land grown from one number (`1dbed87` to `f878351`). On 27 November the owner
said "currently the world feels so random", and the land was given a shape: oceans, continents and
islands (`738d87d`), and harbours with passages between them (`6c4aa93`).

## The hook

He hangs under a glider high over forest, mountains ahead. "This world has no edge. Two weeks ago, it
was one small valley."
