## The story

Rivers and waterfalls came in on 22 November (`0c6dc4e`, day ten): springs in the hills running down to the lakes, stepping down in falls drawn as a flat ribbon. On 27 November the owner: "rivers and waterfall literally need better visual"; redone with white water in ropes, mist, rocks and a pool (`df1e170`). The same evening the owner asked how a waterfall should look and for it to be built to that description: three falls into one pool, wet rock with moss (`2b16521`); and then for them to glow at night (`932d97a`).
