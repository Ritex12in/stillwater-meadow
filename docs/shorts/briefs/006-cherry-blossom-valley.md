## The story

The owner showed a picture of a cherry blossom valley and asked for it to be built (27 November). Its colours were counted against the grove in the game: the picture a third pink and almost no green; the grove two to six per cent pink and a third to two thirds green (docs/endless-world.md). Then step by step: the colour (`76a554d`), stone lanterns on the trails (`ad86a10`), the heart of the grove, a cliff with a fall and pagodas (`65c0529`, `d7cd2eb`), a way in that gives the view (`a93329c`), and the glow at night (`aa550e9`). Every step is filmed from its own version at the same spot. The owner's picture itself is not shown.
