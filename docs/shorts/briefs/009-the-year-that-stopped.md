## The story

The game had a turning year: every two real days a new season; leaves turned, snow came and went, lakes froze. In winter the cherry blossom valley was bare trees in snow (`932d97a`, held at winter). The owner: "winter cherry blossom trees are not looking good, that place is losing its purpose. Remove the season concept, season will be fixed for places" (`0d94bdd`). Now cold country is always winter, a blossom valley always in blossom, everywhere else summer.
