## The story

On day one the mountains were a ridge painted round the one valley (`620f25a`). That evening they were reworked: pointed peaks, snow, a forest in front, still scenery (`df8bfa5`). On 21 November the land lost its edge and the mountains became real ground (`1dbed87`); then they were worn as by water, given a treeline and snow on the faces turned from the sun (`492e523` to `d32cc93`). Now you can climb them and glide off.
